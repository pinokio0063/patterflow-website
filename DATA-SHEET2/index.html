Quick Guide (English)

  • Auto Fill: Type 10*XL in Size cell and press Enter.
  • Range Select: Click a cell, hold Shift and click another.
  • Bulk Sleeve: Select SLV cells and press Enter to fill.
  • Delete: Select cells and press Delete key to clear.
